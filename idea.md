## 🎯 Product Overview & Core Purpose

* **Concept:** Referral-Based Social Network
* **Mission:** Combat loneliness and help introverts build meaningful, real-world social connections.
* **Target Audience:** Primary demographic aged **18–25** (with secondary tiers for older audiences).
* **Business Model:** Hybrid **B2C** (end-users) & **B2B** (event organizers and businesses).

---

## 👥 User Features (B2C Focus)

### 🌿 Social Graph & Network Discovery

* **Connection Tree:** Visual node/tree graph showcasing connections between friends and social circles.
* **Privacy-First Growth:** Connect strictly via invite links, offline real-life meetings, or mutual friend recommendations.
* **Smart Mutual Recommendations:**
* Friends can suggest connections based on calculated compatibility ratings.
* Users retain full control to accept or reject suggestions based on compatibility scores.


* **Progress Sharing:** Share personal milestones and social growth directly with your social tree.

### 🎮 Motivation & Gamification

* **Goal-Based Variable Rewards:** Incentive system designed to motivate introverts to step out and attend events.
* **Social Gamification:** Request points from friends (framed as "helping a friend maintain their streak defense").
* **Brand Mascot:** A unique character to guide users and increase engagement.

### 💬 Messaging & Social Life

* **End-to-End Encrypted Chat:** Secure direct messaging for private chats and community groups.
* **Event & Lifestyle Discovery:**
* Recommendations for local social events, restaurant grand openings, and gym activities.
* Tools to help plan activities and encourage real-world participation.





## 🏢 Business & Organizer Features (B2B Focus)

### 📊 Organizer Dashboard & Event Management

* **Community Management:** Tools for colleges, societies, and local groups to host and maintain communities.
* **Ticketing & Memberships:** Integrated systems for ticket sales and member subscriptions.
* **Event Creation Templates:** Pre-designed digital templates for quick, professional event setup.

### 🎯 Ad Engine & Monetization

* **Targeted Advertising:** Allows organizations to push targeted ads to specific groups based on user interests.
* **Discovery Promotion:** Paid visibility for venue openings, fitness hubs, and local gatherings.
